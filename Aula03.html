Hierarquia

Se não utilizarmos ( ), a ordem na qual a QUERY é executada será:

  1. NOT
  2. AND
  3. OR
Dicas
  • C1 AND (C2 OR C3) = (C1 AND C2) OR (C1 AND C3)
  • C1 OR (C2 AND C3) = (C1 OR C2) AND (C1 OR C3)
  • NOT (C1 AND C2) = (NOT C1) OR (NOT C2)
  • NOT (C1 OR C2) = (NOT C1) AND (NOT C2)
